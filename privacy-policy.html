Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fitness Club ("we", "us" or "our") handles personal data when you visit our website or contact us. We aim to process your data transparently and lawfully in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and applicable law.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Fitness Club, located at 354 Baker Street, London, NW1 6XE, UK.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at info@sportsclub.com.

2. Data we collect

The website does not currently include a registration form or online booking form. We may receive information that you choose to send us, such as your name, email address and message content, when you contact us by email.

When you visit the website, technical data needed for security and operation may be recorded, such as your IP address, device type and basic browser information, depending on our hosting provider and server settings.

3. How we use your data

  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To operate, protect and improve the website.
  • To comply with legal obligations or establish and defend legal claims.

4. Legal basis

Depending on the circumstances, processing is based on taking steps at your request, our legitimate interest in operating a secure website, or a legal obligation. Where consent is required, we will request it before the relevant processing and you may withdraw it at any time.

5. Cookies and third-party services

The website uses basic files and scripts for navigation, animations and content. It may also load third-party resources, including Google Fonts, Font Awesome and the Smooth Scroll library. These providers may process technical data according to their own policies.

We will not use non-essential cookies for advertising or analytics without appropriate notice and consent where required.

6. Sharing and transfers

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only with providers that support the operation of our website or communications, where necessary and with appropriate contractual and technical safeguards, or where required by law.

7. Data retention

We keep data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, as required by law, or to support legal claims. Contact messages are deleted when they are no longer needed for these purposes.

8. Your rights

Subject to the conditions of the GDPR, you may request access to, correction, deletion or restriction of processing of your data, request data portability, or object to particular processing. To exercise a right, email info@sportsclub.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection supervisory authority.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, the law or our practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
